\section{SCP-UNKNOWN}

\objectclass{Safe}

\containment

SCP-TEST-001 is to be stored in a standard containment locker at Site-19. No special precautions are required.

\scpdescription

SCP-TEST-001 is a wooden cube measuring 10cm on each side. When touched by a human, the cube emits a soft humming sound for approximately 30 seconds.

Testing has revealed no harmful effects from exposure to SCP-TEST-001. The source of the humming sound remains unknown.
