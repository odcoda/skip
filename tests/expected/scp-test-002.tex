\section{SCP-UNKNOWN: [[\textgreater{}]]}

\textbf{Object Class:} Euclid

\containment

SCP-TEST-002 requires the following containment measures:

\begin{itemize}
\item A reinforced steel container with walls at least 5cm thick
\item Temperature maintained between 15 and 20 degrees Celsius
\item Weekly inspection by Level 2 personnel
\end{itemize}

Personnel entering the containment area must:

\# Wear protective eyewear
\# Maintain a distance of at least 2 meters
\# Report any unusual behavior immediately

\scpdescription

SCP-TEST-002 is a metallic sphere approximately 15cm in diameter. The sphere exhibits \textit{anomalous gravitational properties}\footnote{First documented in 1987 during initial containment.}, including:

\begin{itemize}
\item Periodic levitation up to 30cm above surfaces
\item Attraction of small metallic objects within a 1-meter radius
\item Emission of low-frequency vibrations detectable by specialized equipment
\end{itemize}

\addendum{TEST-002-A}

Recovery Log

SCP-TEST-002 was recovered from an abandoned warehouse in \blackbox{}\blackbox{}\blackbox{}\blackbox{}\blackbox{}\blackbox{}\blackbox{}\blackbox{}, Ohio on \blackbox{}\blackbox{}/\blackbox{}\blackbox{}/1987. Foundation agents were alerted after local reports of "floating metal balls" appeared in regional newspapers.

\addendum{TEST-002-B}

Incident Report

On \blackbox{}\blackbox{}/\blackbox{}\blackbox{}/1995, SCP-TEST-002 exhibited previously unobserved behavior. The sphere began rotating rapidly and attracted all metallic objects within a 5-meter radius. The incident lasted approximately 3 minutes before the sphere returned to its dormant state.

No personnel were injured during this incident.
