\section{SCP-UNKNOWN: [[\textgreater{}]]}

\textbf{Object Class:} Thaumiel

\containment

SCP-TEST-004 is housed in a specialized chamber at Site-\blackbox{}\blackbox{}. Access requires Level 4 clearance and approval from at least two members of the O5 Council.

\scpdescription

SCP-TEST-004 is an ancient bronze mirror, approximately 45cm in diameter. The mirror's surface displays images of \textbf{potential future events} when activated according to specific ritual protocols.

The accuracy of SCP-TEST-004's predictions varies based on several factors\footnote{See Document TEST-004-ALPHA for complete analysis.}:

\begin{itemize}
\item Temporal distance: Events within 24 hours are shown with 94\% accuracy
\item Complexity: Simple binary outcomes are more reliable than complex scenarios
\item Observer bias: Predictions may be influenced by the observer's expectations
\end{itemize}
