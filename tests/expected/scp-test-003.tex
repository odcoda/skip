\section{SCP-UNKNOWN: [[\textgreater{}]]}

\textbf{Object Class:} Keter

\containment

Due to the nature of SCP-TEST-003, containment focuses on information suppression and rapid response protocols.

\scpdescription

SCP-TEST-003 is an anomalous phenomenon affecting written communication. When specific patterns are written, they trigger temporary memory loss in readers.

\addendum{TEST-003-A}

Interview Log

\begin{scpdialogue}
\logheader{Interview Log}

\speaker{Interviewed} Dr. Sarah Chen

\speaker{Interviewer} Agent Marcus Webb

\speaker{Date} \blackbox{}\blackbox{}/\blackbox{}\blackbox{}/2015

\speaker{Agent Webb} Please describe what happened on the day of the incident.

\speaker{Dr. Chen} I was reviewing the documents in Lab 7. Around 14:00, I noticed something odd about the formatting.

\speaker{Agent Webb} Can you be more specific?

\speaker{Dr. Chen} The words seemed to... shift. Like they were rearranging themselves on the page.

\speaker{Agent Webb} And then?

\speaker{Dr. Chen} I don't remember. The next thing I knew, it was three hours later and I was in the medical bay.

\speaker{Agent Webb} Thank you, Dr. Chen. That will be all for now.

\end{scpdialogue}
